\subsection{Correct-message acceptance and erasures}

Figure~\ref{fig:acceptance} and
Tables~\ref{tab:cells-average} and \ref{tab:cells-peak} separate the two
power constraints while retaining all three profiles and six reference
levels. Their confidence intervals are pointwise, not simultaneous bands.
The equal-weight grid summary has 1232 correct acceptances in 2304 records
(53.5\%) under unit-average power and 1088/2304 (47.2\%) under unit-peak
power. For quiet, moderate and disturbed respectively, the unit-average
totals are 407/768, 417/768 and 408/768; the unit-peak totals are 365/768,
364/768 and 359/768. These fractions summarize the deliberately balanced
grid, not an operating distribution of HF conditions.

At $-24\dB$, quiet fading under unit-average power has two correct
acceptances; the other five conditions have none. At $-14\dB$, five
conditions accept all 128 messages and quiet fading under unit-peak power
accepts 125/128. Even a 128/128 cell has a Wilson interval of approximately
$[0.971,1]$, not proof of error-free operation. No incorrect message is
accepted in any of the 4608 signal records; the other outcomes are erasures.
Because the power settings are paired, these are not 4608 independent
opportunities for an undetected error.

\subsection{The 50\% acceptance crossing}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering\small
\input{study/publication-thresholds.tex}
\caption{Interpolated 50\% crossing in reference-level dB and conservative
95\% grid brackets under a monotone-response assumption. Each bracket
uses six simultaneous exact binomial intervals for its own curve;
coverage is not joint across the six curves.}
\label{tab:thresholds}
\end{table}

All six crossings interpolate between the measured $-20$ and $-18\dB$
points. At unit-average power they are approximately $-19.3$, $-19.5$
and $-19.3\dB$ for quiet, moderate and disturbed. All three unit-peak
values round to $-18.7\dB$. The quiet unit-average grid bracket is
$[-22,-18]\dB$; the other five are $[-20,-18]\dB$.
These overlapping brackets and the 2\,dB measurement spacing do not
support a ranking based on the small interpolated differences.

The observed curves also change order across the grid. For example,
unit-average quiet and disturbed have 24 and 4 correct acceptances at
$-22\dB$, but 91 and 114 at $-18\dB$. The ITU profile names identify
delay/Doppler parameters rather than a universal ordering of this receiver's
frame-acceptance probability. The experiment changes both parameters and
does not isolate a cause for the observed differences.

All 24 sampled exact Cartesian corners succeed. At $-20\dB$, one raw
correct decode is rejected by the evidence gate; the other unsuccessful
cases are raw-decoder failures. No incorrect message is accepted. These
results exercise the stated ideal-frontend geometry, not every possible
noise or fading realization at a boundary.

The separate noise check with alternating frontend centers has zero raw
and zero accepted decodes in 4096 captures, with two evidence-gate
exceedances. Its own one-sided 95\% upper acceptance bound is
$7.31\times10^{-4}$ per capture. It is not pooled with the centered
frontend check in Table~\ref{tab:noise}, because the input ensembles differ.

Bias is mean estimated SNR minus the configured value; RMSE is the root
mean squared difference. In the unconditional AWGN rows, bias ranges from
$-0.1459$ to $-0.0315\dB$ and RMSE from 0.2181 to 0.8357\,dB.

realized receive-power fluctuations relative to transmit-reference SNR.
Their biases range from $-0.5810$ to $0.0190\dB$ and RMSE from 0.8194
to 2.0109\,dB. The valid-case counts are therefore essential to interpreting
these figures. No empirical bias correction is fitted.

\subsection{Waveform duration, bandwidth, and peak power}

The active waveform has exactly 116,800 samples at 12\,kHz, including both
filter tails, for a duration of 9.733333333\,s. The physical summary uses
2304 distinct randomized source cases, counted once each despite the
paired power records. Mean $B_{99}$ is 1959.236\,Hz and the largest
observed value is 1998.225\,Hz. Mean PAPR is 0.710906\,dB and the largest
observed value is 0.710965\,dB. These extrema belong to the sampled payloads,
not to an exhaustive search over the codebook. Unit-peak active-frame mean
power is approximately 0.8490 and energy 8.2636 in normalized power-seconds,
compared with 1 and 9.7333 under unit-average normalization.
