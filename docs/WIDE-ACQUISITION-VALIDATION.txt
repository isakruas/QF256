Frontend contract
-----------------

The target iSDR USB channel is 300..4000 Hz and is mixed to complex baseband
about 2150 Hz.  Its nominal baseband is therefore -1850..+1850 Hz.  The
validation model applies the ideal complex mask |f| <= 1850 Hz.

The QF256 tone centers extend to +/-996.09375 Hz at zero CFO.  At the public
CFO limits they extend to +/-1746.09375 Hz, leaving 103.90625 Hz between the
outer tone center and the nominal LP1850 edge.  This is why LP1850, rather
than LP1500 or LP1750, is the registered frontend model.  The ideal mask does
not model passband ripple or a real filter transition band; recorded iSDR IQ
remains a separate end-to-end check.

LP1500 was also measured as an out-of-contract stress.  It cannot retain the
complete waveform over the +/-750 Hz CFO contract.  Its 2048-case calibration
gave p99=33.097599247021122, 82/2048 evidence values at or above the retained
gate, raw CRC=0, and selected-N0/true-N0 as low as 0.7914148670988151.  Those
numbers are not used to set the operational gate.  The low ratio occurs when
the acquired noise hypothesis moves some of the 256 tone projections outside
the too-narrow passband.


Receiver under test
-------------------

Acquisition uses three stages:

  coarse: start step 96 samples; CFO step 3.90625 Hz over +/-750 Hz
  middle: start step 16 over +/-96 around the coarse result;
          CFO step 0.244140625 Hz over +/-3.90625 around it
  fine:   start step 4 over +/-16 around the middle result;
          CFO step 0.0244140625 Hz over +/-0.244140625 around it

All local intervals are clipped to the public timing and CFO limits.

The legacy N0 estimate uses spectral guards outside the modem passband.  A USB
frontend removes those guards.  After acquisition, the fallback sorts the 256
tone-projection energies separately for each of the 60 data symbols, takes the
mean of order statistics 128 and 129, divides by the exact exponential-sample
expectation 0.6951041202281072, and averages the 60 corrected medians.  This
tone estimate is selected only when guard_N0 < 0.5*tone_N0.  Full-band inputs
therefore preserve the guard estimator; LP1850 inputs select the robust
in-band estimator.


Noise method
------------

For each case the generator makes independent complex AWGN with

  N0 = 1/(2500*10^(SNR/10))
  sigma per real component = sqrt(12000*N0/2).

Reference SNR cycles through -24, -22, -20, -18, -16 and -14 dB.  A NumPy FFT,
an inclusive |f| <= 1850 mask, and an inverse FFT create the band-limited
complex capture.  The result is converted to interleaved complex float32 and
passed to qf256_rx_cf32 in blind mode.  Noise captures contain 180000 complex
samples.  Separate worker processes are used because the library is not
thread-safe within one process.

The scalar selected-N0 ratio below is

  result.noise_variance/(12000*true_N0).

Quantiles use Hyndman-Fan type 7: h=(n-1)*p, followed by linear interpolation
between zero-based order statistics floor(h) and floor(h)+1.

Signal method and results
-------------------------

The signal run uses 128 distinct payload/noise cases at each of -20 and -18 dB.
The seed is 8086101100+i at -20 dB and 8087101100+i at -18 dB.  Each receiver
capture is exactly 125200 samples.  Cases 0..3 at each SNR are the Cartesian
corners

  (start,CFO) = (3600,-750), (3600,+750), (8400,-750), (8400,+750).

The remaining starts are uniform integers in [3600,8400], CFO is uniform in
[-750,+750], phase is uniform in [-pi,+pi], and payload bits are independent.

To avoid FFT circular wrap at the start=8400 boundary, the signal and
continuous noise are placed in a 262144-sample filtering context.  The ideal
LP1850 mask is applied there, and the central 125200 samples are passed to the
receiver.  The context leaves 68472 samples on each side of the capture.  In
particular, start 8400 plus the 116800-sample frame ends exactly at receiver
sample 125200 without placing the filter wrap at that boundary.

Gate decision
-------------

The retained value 30.220758775848424 is the full-band 2048-case calibration
p99 and is greater than the LP1850 calibration p99.  It yields a 0.9521%
evidence exceedance rate on the independent LP1850 check, with zero raw CRCs
and zero accepted noise frames.  Lowering it to the LP1850 calibration p99
would raise independent LP1850 evidence exceedance to 1.2207%.  Raising it is
not supported by the false-acceptance observations and would discard another
valid weak signal.  At the retained value the measured signal cost was one of
118 raw-correct -20 dB frames and zero of 128 at -18 dB.


Reproduction
------------

The versioned harness is:

  study/validate_wide_acquisition.py

It requires Python 3 and NumPy, loads the public C ABI, verifies the 152-byte
result layout, declares the two C entry points as void, and rejects nonzero API
statuses or an API-accepted value inconsistent with CRC and the supplied gate
during summary.  The registered run used Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.2.4,
GNU Fortran 14.2.0 and x86-64 Debian.  OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS and OMP_NUM_THREADS
were fixed at one in every worker process.

Build the current library, choose output paths that do not exist, then run:

  make -j4 all

  OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u \
    study/validate_wide_acquisition.py noise --lib lib/libqf256.so \
    --count 2048 --seed-base 6066101100 --cutoff 1850 --workers 4 \
    --output study/build/wide-cal.csv

  OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u \
    study/validate_wide_acquisition.py noise --lib lib/libqf256.so \
    --count 4096 --seed-base 7076101100 --cutoff 1850 --workers 4 \
    --output study/build/wide-check.csv

  OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u \
    study/validate_wide_acquisition.py signal --lib lib/libqf256.so \
    --count-per-level 128 --snr -20 -18 --seed-base 8086101100 \
    --cutoff 1850 --workers 8 --chunk 4 \
    --output study/build/wide-signals.csv

Summarize without changing the records:

  python3 study/validate_wide_acquisition.py summarize-noise \
    --input study/build/wide-cal.csv --gate 30.220758775848424
  python3 study/validate_wide_acquisition.py summarize-noise \
    --input study/build/wide-check.csv --gate 30.220758775848424
  python3 study/validate_wide_acquisition.py summarize-signal \
    --input study/build/wide-signals.csv --gate 30.220758775848424

Alternatively, `make wide-validation` builds the library, runs the three
registered batches and prints their summaries under
study/build/wide-acquisition.  The target is intentionally not a dependency of
`test` or `strict`; it performs 6400 blind receiver calls.  Set
WIDE_VALIDATION_WORKERS to control process parallelism.  The harness refuses
to overwrite a CSV and checks this before starting the expensive calls.  Use a
fresh directory when retaining an earlier run, for example:

  make WIDE_VALIDATION_DIR=study/build/wide-acquisition-run2 wide-validation
