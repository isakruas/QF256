QF256 CONVENTIONAL-HF ACQUISITION VALIDATION
=============================================

Status
------

Validation date: 2026-10-11
Sample rate: 12000 complex samples/s
Synchronization mode: QF256_SYNC_BLIND_HF (2)
Blind timing interval: samples 3600 through 8400, inclusive
Blind CFO interval: -125 through +125 Hz, inclusive
Public evidence gate: 30.220758775848424 (unchanged)

The additive blind-HF profile was introduced for the iSDR conventional USB
channel.  The existing QF256_SYNC_BLIND mode and its -750..+750 Hz interval
are unchanged.  This validation does not lower or recalibrate the public gate.


Frontend contract
-----------------

The physical audio passband is 300..2700 Hz and its nominal centre is 1500 Hz.
The selectable decoder/transmitter centre is 1475..1525 Hz.  Relative to the
selected centre, the two limiting ideal complex masks are therefore

  centre 1475 Hz (offset -25):  -1175..+1225 Hz
  centre 1525 Hz (offset +25):  -1225..+1175 Hz

The QF256 outer tone centres are +/-996.09375 Hz.  Combining centre offsets
+/-25 Hz and residual CFO +/-125 Hz places the outer physical tone centres at
353.90625 and 2646.09375 Hz in the worst Cartesian corners.  Each limiting
tone centre is therefore 53.90625 Hz inside the nominal USB passband.

The validation applies the corresponding ideal FFT masks.  It does not model
passband ripple or a physical filter transition band; recorded iSDR audio/IQ
and the implemented channel filter remain separate end-to-end checks.


Receiver under test
-------------------

The blind-HF acquisition uses the same three timing/frequency stages as the
wide receiver, clipped to the narrower public CFO interval:

  coarse: start step 96 samples; CFO step 3.90625 Hz over +/-125 Hz
  middle: start step 16 over +/-96 around the coarse result;
          CFO step 0.244140625 Hz over +/-3.90625 around it
  fine:   start step 4 over +/-16 around the middle result;
          CFO step 0.0244140625 Hz over +/-0.244140625 around it

The 250 Hz coarse interval contains exactly 64 steps, so both -125 and +125 Hz
are evaluated.  The legacy N0 guards at +/-2500..5000 Hz are absent after this
frontend.  The receiver therefore selects the robust in-band estimator formed
from the middle two of 256 tone-projection energies per data symbol when the
guard estimate is less than half the tone estimate.


Noise method and results
------------------------

The check contains 4096 independent, 180000-sample complex-AWGN captures.
Reference noise levels cycle through -24, -22, -20, -18, -16 and -14 dB using

  N0 = 1/(2500*10^(SNR/10))
  sigma per real component = sqrt(12000*N0/2).

For zero-based index i the seed is 9096101100+i.  Frontend offsets alternate
-25,+25 Hz.  Each filtered capture is passed to qf256_rx_cf32 with
QF256_SYNC_BLIND_HF.  No threshold is selected from this check batch.

  cases:                              4096
  type-7 evidence p99:                26.919677808080525
  evidence >= retained gate:          3
  raw CRC:                            0
  accepted:                           0
  selected-N0 ratio min/median/max:   0.95405259858109392 /
                                      0.99788638963096166 /
                                      1.041697049740306
  maximum evidence:                   32.539004062544016

For zero accepted events in 4096 independent captures, the exact one-sided
95% binomial upper bound is 0.0007311125564753995 (0.0731113%) per capture.
This bound concerns full acceptance (CRC and evidence), not evidence-only
threshold crossings.


Signal method and results
-------------------------

There are 128 distinct payload/noise cases at each of -20, -18 and -4 dB.
The seed bases are 9097101100, 9098101100 and 9099101100 respectively.  Each
receiver capture has 125200 samples.  The first eight cases at every SNR are
the Cartesian corners of

  start = 3600 or 8400 samples
  CFO = -125 or +125 Hz
  frontend offset = -25 or +25 Hz.

Remaining starts are uniform integers in [3600,8400], CFO is uniform in
[-125,+125], phase is uniform in [-pi,+pi], payload bits are independent, and
frontend offsets alternate -25,+25 Hz.  Signal and continuous noise are placed
in a 262144-sample filtering context before the central capture is extracted,
so FFT circular wrap remains 68472 samples away from both capture boundaries.

Success below requires raw CRC, the original payload, and evidence at or above
the unchanged public gate.

  SNR       cases    raw correct    correct and evidence >= gate
  -20 dB      128       113                       112
  -18 dB      128       128                       128
   -4 dB      128       128                       128

Corner result: all 24 Cartesian-corner cases (eight at every SNR) recovered
the original payload and passed the unchanged public gate.


Decision and limits
-------------------

The fixed batch supports the blind-HF profile for its stated software and
ideal-frontend contract: no noise capture was accepted, every exact
timing/CFO/frontend corner was accepted, and all 128 cases were accepted at
-18 and -4 dB.  At -20 dB, 113 payloads passed raw CRC and 112 also passed the
unchanged evidence gate.  The single raw decode below the retained gate is
recorded rather than used to retune the gate.

These are fixed-seed AWGN and ideal-frontend results.  They validate the
closed acquisition/frontend geometry and its software acceptance rule; they
do not guarantee an on-air decode probability.  Antenna efficiency, external
interference, impulsive noise, AGC behaviour, multipath, Doppler fading, and a
real filter transition band are outside this batch.


Reproduction
------------

The versioned harness is study/validate_wide_acquisition.py.  Its default
profile remains the wide +/-750 Hz receiver.  Reproduce this HF check with:

  make -j4 all
  make HF_VALIDATION_WORKERS=8 hf-validation

Equivalent indexed batch commands are:

  OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u \
    study/validate_wide_acquisition.py noise --lib lib/libqf256.so \
    --profile hf --count 4096 --seed-base 9096101100 --cutoff 1200 \
    --frontend-offset -25 25 --workers 8 \
    --output study/build/hf-acquisition/noise-check.csv

  OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -u \
    study/validate_wide_acquisition.py signal --lib lib/libqf256.so \
    --profile hf --count-per-level 128 --snr -20 -18 -4 \
    --seed-base 9097101100 --cutoff 1200 --frontend-offset -25 25 \
    --workers 8 --chunk 4 \
    --output study/build/hf-acquisition/signals.csv
