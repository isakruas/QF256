QF256 verification
==================

Receiver contract
-----------------
Sample rate: 12000 complex samples/s.
Automatic acquisition: blind, starts 3600..8400 samples and CFO -125..125 Hz.
Aligned-input test setting: known, start zero and residual CFO zero.
Acceptance: raw FEC/CRC success and evidence >= 30.220758775848424.
The gate is a fixed design constant, not estimated from the published trials.

Reproduction
------------
Run from the repository root:

  make -j4
  make test test-c-api examples-check
  make strict
  make study-analysis
  make report

The strict target rebuilds the modem, tests, and study analyzers with Fortran
and C warnings treated as errors, runs regressions and examples, and
regenerates study summaries. It does not clear campaign directories.
The study-analysis target reads published records; it does not rerun channel
trials. Full campaign commands and seed formulas are in study/README.md and
study/MODEL.md. Build logs are local generated artifacts, not measurement data.

Study design
------------
The principal campaign evaluates the quiet, moderate and disturbed
mid-latitude two-path profiles specified by ITU-R F.1487-0, Annex 3,
sections 3.1-3.3, six transmit-reference SNR levels, and two paired power
settings, with 128 cases per cell. Its 36 cells contain 4608 records for
2304 randomized cases. Profile selection does not imply compliance with
the recommendation's complete BER and test-duration procedure; each fade
uses 64 spectral lines.
All 77 payload bits are sampled. Signal and noise pass together through
an ideal 300..2700 Hz frontend centered at 1500 Hz. Individual fades are
not power-normalized. Main-study CFO is sampled over -2..2 Hz; acquisition
boundaries are covered by a separate AWGN geometry experiment.

The 2048-capture noise diagnostic lot does not set the gate. A separately
seeded 4096-capture check provides the principal noise-only acceptance
measurement. The geometry experiment uses 384 signal captures and 4096
noise captures through the two limiting 1475/1525 Hz frontend centers.
The two noise checks concern different input ensembles and are not pooled.

Per-trial outcomes, statistical intervals, and waveform summaries are in
study/. The technical report specifies their interpretation and limitations.
The receiver SNR check has 15 rows of 128 attempted estimates: six AWGN
levels and three levels for each fading profile, or 1920 attempts. It uses
known aligned tones in AWGN and correct accepted messages in fading;
these differently conditioned populations are reported separately.
None of these software or simulated-channel checks guarantees
an on-air decoding probability, practical filter response, or immunity to
unmodeled interference.

Integrity
---------
Before regenerating published artifacts:

  sha256sum -c docs/DELIVERY.sha256
  sha256sum -c docs/CORE.sha256
  sha256sum -c study/DATA.sha256
  sha256sum -c study/PHYSICAL.sha256

The delivery manifest covers the distribution except itself. The core
manifest identifies the numerical implementation. Study manifests identify
the input records and physical measurements used by the analysis.
Acquisition CSVs include CPU latency; their hashes identify the published
execution, not an exact replay promise. Compare deterministic decode fields
without latency when rerunning that experiment.
