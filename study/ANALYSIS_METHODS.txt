QF256 statistical analysis

The signal/noise records are generated by study/run_campaign.py and
the public receiver. The standalone Fortran analyzer reads completed records:
make study-analysis. It does not execute a waveform/channel/noise simulation.
The physical summarizer additionally checks all paired waveform measurements.

Provenance and completeness

CAMPAIGN.json records the executed source/executable hashes, compiler version,
flags, fixed design, seeds, start/completion times and canonical output hashes.
DATA_SOURCES.csv maps S01..S10 to deterministic source shards produced by the
runner under study/build/campaign. confirmation.csv/noise.csv preserve source
ID and contiguous source row. Each shard can be rebuilt from indexed calls;
it is not an external input. DATA.sha256 covers the canonical records.
S01..S03 are quiet/moderate/disturbed under average power, S04..S06 are
the corresponding peak-power records, S07..S08 diagnostic noise, and
S09..S10 independent-check noise.

The analyzer requires all 36 channel/power/SNR cells, 128 records per cell,
exactly once, and all noise indices exactly once. It rejects nonfinite parsed
numbers, incorrect seeds, malformed payload tokens, inconsistent outcomes,
acceptance below the fixed gate, invalid iteration/synchronization bounds,
missing/duplicate rows, and broken power-pair payload/timing/CFO identities.
tests/test_study.py verifies representative negative cases.

Signal design

The grid is quiet/moderate/disturbed x average/peak x
[-24,-22,-20,-18,-16,-14] dB. The channel settings are the mid-latitude
profiles in ITU-R F.1487-0, Annex 3, sections 3.1-3.3; their finite-line
implementation and its limits are specified in MODEL.md.
The two power constraints share 2,304 payload/channel/noise cases, giving
4,608 paired records, not 4,608 mutually independent cases. All 77 payload
bits are uniform; 20-digit hexadecimal tokens retain leading zeroes.
Source/channel/noise seed formulae and the ideal frontend are in MODEL.md.
Reference SNR is based on unit transmitted power and 2500 Hz. Actual
average-power SNR and Eb/N0 are recorded separately for peak-limited trials.
Faded receive power is never renormalized.

A row is correct accepted, missed (no accepted message), or accepted wrong.
Correct includes public operational acceptance and exact payload recovery.
Accepted wrong is not a count of decoder CRC failures. All three categories
sum to 128 in each cell. Counts, including any accepted wrong, are reported
without outcome filtering.

Cell intervals and aggregates

cells.csv gives separate pointwise two-sided 95% Wilson intervals for each
event probability in each cell. For n=128, p=k/n, z=1.959963984540054:
d=1+z^2/n; center=(p+z^2/(2n))/d;
radius=z*sqrt(p*(1-p)/n+z^2/(4n^2))/d.
Endpoints are clipped to [0,1]. These are not simultaneous bounds across
all categories or cells.

aggregate.csv contains descriptive equal-weight fixed-grid totals only,
without pooled-binomial confidence intervals. The conditions have different
event probabilities and do not represent an operational distribution of HF
conditions. The paired-power total is explicitly not an independent count.

50% crossing and uncertainty

Each curve's point estimate interpolates adjacent observed 2 dB SNR points
bracketing correct-acceptance probability 0.5. It is a descriptive convention,
not a subgrid experiment. The analyzer verifies observed monotonicity.

Six Clopper-Pearson intervals per curve use tail probability 0.05/(2*6).
Bonferroni gives at least 95% simultaneous coverage within that curve.
The grid-bracket lower end is the greatest SNR with upper probability bound
below 0.5; the upper end is the least SNR with lower bound above 0.5.
Under a monotone true response and a crossing within the tested range this
is a conservative 95% grid bracket, not an interpolated endpoint interval.
Coverage is per curve, not jointly across all six curves. Table rounding
does not justify accuracy finer than the grid.

Exact bounds use bisection of binomial tails evaluated using log-gamma.

Diagnostic noise and independent check

The 2,048 diagnostic captures and 4,096 independent check captures contain
only Gaussian noise through the same ideal frontend; each observation is
15 seconds. Labels [-24,-22,-20,-18,-16,-14] specify noise density N0, not SNR
in noise without a signal. Both lots use the fixed public gate
30.220758775848424. The diagnostic type-7 99th percentile is reported only
as a description; it does not define, calibrate or replace the gate.

Operational acceptance is raw CRC/FEC success AND evidence at least the
gate. Evidence-only threshold exceedances are not accepted messages.
noise_summary.csv supplies one-sided 95% exact bounds only for the independent
check. The diagnostic lot is descriptive. No threshold is retuned after
inspection of either lot, the fading grid or acquisition-boundary results.

For zero events in n independent captures the one-sided 95% upper probability
is 1-exp(log(0.05)/n); for n=4096 this is 0.00073111255647539952.
The pooled zero-event formula is exact for a common probability, and
conservative for an allocation-weighted mean when noise-scale probabilities
differ: product(1-p_i) <= (1-mean(p_i))^n. Per-scale bounds are pointwise,
not simultaneous. Zero raw CRC events do not measure a benefit caused by
the gate; report both raw and operational counts.

Random-stream scope

The five principal role sets are numerically disjoint: 2,304 source,
2,304 fading, 2,304 additive signal-noise, 2,048 diagnostic and 4,096 independent
noise seeds. Average/peak cases deliberately share streams. Pseudorandom
stream independence is a modeling assumption, not established solely by
different seed integers. Compiler/runtime and exact draw order are retained.
The SNR validation adds 768 AWGN-noise seeds, 1,152 fading seeds and
1,152 fading-noise seeds. All 16,128 Fortran trial-seed integers across
these eight roles are distinct; deliberate reuse between paired power
settings is counted once.

SNR and acquisition checks

SNR_VALIDATION.csv distinguishes unconditional known-payload AWGN estimates
from estimates conditional on accepted-correct fading decodes; its valid-case
counts are part of the result. Bias is measured against transmitted reference
SNR, not realized faded power. No empirical correction is fitted.
The 15 rows contain 128 attempted estimates each: six AWGN levels and
three levels (-18, -16, -14 dB) for each of quiet, moderate and disturbed.
The 1,920 attempted estimates are separate from the 4,608 signal outcomes.

The separate acquisition experiment uses all 77 random bits and tests
physical frontend-centre and exact acquisition-start/CFO corners in AWGN.
Its results must not be combined with the fading grid as independent samples
from one probability. See MODEL.md and acquisition/*.json for design/seeds.
